Download

Safari on iPhone will only save these one at a time, and puts them in Files. Doing this on a computer is far less fiddly.

Then
  1. Put all four files in your repo next to index.html, replacing the existing ones. Keep the filenames exactly as they are.
  2. Wait for the green tick on the Actions tab, then load your site in Safari and hard-refresh.
  3. Delete the existing icon from your home screen and add it again from the Share menu. This step is the one people miss — iOS copies the icon when you add the shortcut and never checks again.

If the icon still comes out as a tiny screenshot of the page, iOS isn't finding the file. Open your-site-url/apple-touch-icon.png directly in Safari — if that 404s, the file isn't where the page expects it.
