Your origin

This exact string is what belongs under Authorized JavaScript origins on the client. Scheme and host only — no path, no trailing slash.

Does the client exist?

This sends your ID straight to Google's sign-in endpoint, bypassing the app entirely. You're not trying to complete sign-in — you're reading which error comes back, because the two possible errors mean completely different things.

Reading the result
"The OAuth client was not found" · 401 invalid_client

The client does not exist in Google's records. Not an origin problem, not a consent problem. Either it was never created, it was deleted, or it lives in a project you're not looking at. Go to APIs & Services → Credentials and check the OAuth 2.0 Client IDs section specifically — in the newer console this sits under Google Auth Platform → Clients. Seeing your project, your consent screen, or your enabled APIs tells you nothing about whether a client exists; they're separate objects. If the list is empty, create one: Create credentials → OAuth client ID → Web application.

"Error 400: redirect_uri_mismatch"

Good news, despite looking like a failure. Google found the client and moved on to its next check — so your ID is correct. This test doesn't register a redirect URI, so this error is the expected pass. Your problem is elsewhere: most likely your origin isn't listed on the client, or the app is running an old cached copy of itself.

A Google account picker, or "this app isn't verified"

Everything is registered correctly. The unverified warning is normal for a personal project in Testing mode — continue past it. Back out of this screen without approving; you don't need to finish here.

"Error 403: access_denied"

The client is fine, but your Google account isn't on the list. Add yourself under Test users on the consent screen.

If the ID checks out but the app still fails

Then the app is running an old copy of itself. The service worker serves from cache until its version string changes. Open sw.js in your repo, change daily-card-v1 to daily-card-v2, commit, wait for the green tick on Actions, then delete the home screen shortcut and load the site fresh in Safari.
